# Spelling Garden 🐝🌸

A spelling practice game for young kids, built for iPad Safari (works on any modern browser).

- **Listen & Learn** — hear a word, watch its letters drop in one by one while each letter is read aloud, then hear it in a sentence.
- **Build It** — hear the word and tap honeycomb letter tiles into place (with a couple of extra letters mixed in). Tiles can grow a word up to a bud; typing it makes it bloom.
- **Spell It!** — hear the word and spell it on a big on-screen keyboard. Two tries with green-letter hints, then the game spells it with you and you copy it.
- **Bee Challenge** — 20 words with no hints. A live score shows how many are done and correct, with a small toast for each right answer. At the end, an outcome toast and a podium against four bot players whose scores stay close to your best. Optional timer, best score per list, and a "practice my misses" button.
- Stars, honey drops, a daily streak, and a garden where every mastered word blooms. Missed words come back sooner.

Pick a look with 🎨 or on the Bee Challenge screen: Garden, Magic Castle, Kolkata, Christmas, or Halloween. Themes change the scenery and rewards, not the words.

**No word lists are included.** A grown-up adds words on the device (press and hold ⚙️): paste them or load a `.txt` file. Format:

```
# Week 12
kite | My kite flew up high.
green | The frog is green.
```

Everything is stored in the browser's local storage on that device. Add to Home Screen for full-screen, offline play.
