# Spelling Garden 🐝🌸

A spelling practice game for young kids, built for iPad Safari (works on any modern browser).

- **Listen & Learn** — hear a word, watch its letters drop in one by one while each letter is read aloud, then hear it in a sentence.
- **Build It** — hear the word and tap honeycomb letter tiles into place (with a couple of extra letters mixed in). Tiles can grow a word up to a bud; typing it makes it bloom.
- **Spell It!** — hear the word and spell it on a big on-screen keyboard. Two tries with green-letter hints, then the game spells it with you and you copy it.
- **Bee Challenge** — five levels of 20 fixed words with no hints, built from the two main lists. A mistake shows the right spelling, and missed or skipped words come back at the end; only words still wrong then lose a point. Score 80% to unlock the next level. A live score shows how many are done and correct, with a small toast for each right answer. At the end, an outcome toast and a podium against four bot players whose scores stay close to your best. Optional timer, best score per list, and a "practice my misses" button.
- Stars, honey drops, a daily streak, a home-screen path of levels, and a count of mastered words. Missed words come back sooner.

Pick a look with 🎨 or on the Bee Challenge screen: Garden, Magic Castle, Kolkata, Christmas, or Halloween. Themes change the scenery and rewards, not the words.

Two characters beside each word card cheer on right answers and look sad on wrong ones, and change with the theme.

Each session starts by asking who's playing. Every player has their own progress, honey, streak, best scores, and report card on that device. The grown-ups corner (⚙️) shows the report card and holds Start over.

**No word lists are included.** A grown-up adds words on the device (tap ⚙️): paste them or load a `.txt` file. Format:

```
# Week 12
kite | My kite flew up high.
green | The frog is green.
```

Everything is stored in the browser's local storage on that device. Add to Home Screen for full-screen, offline play.
