How to play

Getting started

  1. Add it to your iPad. Open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It then opens full-screen like an app and works offline. It also runs in any web browser.
  2. Type your child’s name. The game asks who’s playing each time it opens. Each child keeps their own progress.
  3. Add the words. Tap ⚙️, then Load a file or paste the words in. The game comes with no words, so this step is needed once on each device.
  4. Turn the sound on, including the iPad’s silent switch. The voice is a big part of the game.

Word file format

A plain text file. Put a tag line above each list, then one word per line. A practice sentence after a bar is optional.

# Medium Girl
kite | My kite flew up high.
green | The frog is green.

# Big Girl
garden | We planted seeds in the garden.

# Week 12
cat, dog, sun

Use the tags # Medium Girl and # Big Girl with 50 words each to get all five Bee Challenge levels. Any other tag, like # Week 12, makes its own practice round.

The games

  • 🏆 Bee Challenge: the main game. It works like a real spelling bee, with 20 words and no hints.
  • 👂 Listen & Learn: hear a word, watch it spelled letter by letter, then hear it in a sentence. Best for new words.
  • 🧩 Build It: hear a word and put letter tiles in order. A couple of extra letters are mixed in.
  • ✏️ Spell It: hear a word and type it. Two tries with hints, then the game shows the spelling and your child copies it.

A good session: Learn, then Build, then Spell, with a Bee Challenge every couple of days. About 10 minutes is plenty.

Bee Challenge rules

  • Mistakes: the game shows and says the right spelling, then brings the word back at the end.
  • ⏭️ Skip for now: saves a hard word for the end.
  • End round: each missed or skipped word gets one more try. A point is lost only if it’s still wrong then.
  • Levels: 5 levels of 20 fixed words, from easier to harder. Score 16 out of 20 (80%) to unlock the next one.
  • Podium: your child races four pretend players whose scores stay close to their best. You can rename them in ⚙️.
  • Timer: optional. It counts up and never ends the game.

Stars, honey and blooms

Right answers earn stars and honey, and daily play builds a 🔥 streak. The home screen shows the five levels and a count of words in bloom. A word blooms once it’s typed right on the first try three times. Tap 🎨 on the home screen to change the look.

Reports for grown-ups

Tap ⚙️ to see the Report card. Pick a child, then tap a level to see every word:

  • ✅ First try: spelled right straight away.
  • 🔁 Fixed on 2nd try: wrong first, then corrected.
  • ❌ Wrong: still wrong after the second try.

The report stays until you tap Start over in ⚙️. Change player and Remove player are there too.

Your privacy

Names, words and progress stay in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere, so progress doesn’t move between devices. Removing the app from the Home Screen deletes it.

Word lists

Words stay on this device only. One word per line, with an optional sentence after a bar: kite | My kite flew up high. Tag each list with a line above its words: # Medium Girl, # Big Girl, or a new name like # Week 12. One file can hold them all. Commas work too: cat, dog, sun.
